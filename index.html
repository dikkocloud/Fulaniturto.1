More than vocabulary.

Fulfulde carries the memory of a people who crossed the Sahel with their cattle, their poetry, and their code of Pulaaku. Learning it is learning how to greet an elder, how to tell a story, how to belong.

Fulaniturto teaches Fulfulde the way it was passed down — through conversation, proverbs, and real cultural context — structured for anyone learning it fresh, wherever in the world they are.
