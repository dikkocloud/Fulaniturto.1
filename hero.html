Why it matters

More than vocabulary.

Fulfulde carries the memory of a people who crossed the Sahel with their cattle, their poetry, and their code of Pulaaku. Learning it is learning how to greet an elder, how to tell a story, how to belong.

01

Spoken first

Fulaniturto teaches Fulfulde the way it was passed down — through conversation, from greetings to fluency.

02

Rooted in culture

Proverbs, oral tradition, and real cultural context sit alongside every lesson, so words never float free of meaning.

03

Learn from a native

Structured for anyone learning fresh, wherever in the world they are, with direct access to your teacher.
